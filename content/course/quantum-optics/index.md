Quantum Optics studies light and its interaction with matter at the quantum level. The course builds from field quantization and photon states toward modern topics in quantum technologies.

Core topics include:

- quantization of the electromagnetic field;
- coherent, squeezed, and other nonclassical states of light;
- photodetection and quantum correlation functions;
- atom-light interaction and the Jaynes-Cummings and quantum Rabi models;
- cavity quantum electrodynamics;
- dissipation, master equations, and open quantum systems;
- selected applications in quantum information and quantum sensing.

The course aims to connect formal derivations, physical interpretation, and representative calculations from current research.
