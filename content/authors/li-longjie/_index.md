Longjie Li is an MSc student who entered in 2024, working on quantum chaos and quantum optics.

My research focuses on exploring the quantum analogues of classical chaos. By employing different diagnostic tools that characterize chaotic behavior, I aim to identify the physical origin of chaos in quantum systems. The central idea is to seek simple principles in a complex world and stable laws underlying seemingly disordered evolution.
