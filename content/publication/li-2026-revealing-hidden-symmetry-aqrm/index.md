summary: "A physical construction of hidden parity at integer bias, with a spin-resolved interference probe for crossings."
abstract: "At integer bias in the asymmetric quantum Rabi model, exact crossings return even though ordinary parity is broken. The authors derive the conserved charge by starting from the exchange of resonant partners on two displaced oscillator ladders and compensating for the tunneling that changes that exchange. For nonzero coupling and tunneling, the sign of the charge separates states into two sectors. The work also shows how spin-resolved mirror interference can reveal the ladder offset and, together with the charge magnitude, predict how a small bias detuning mixes the crossing states."
